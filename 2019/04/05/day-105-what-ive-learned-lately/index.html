Day 105: What I’ve Learned Lately

5 April 20195 เมษายน 20192019年4月5日

Grammar only. The title is unchanged.แก้แต่ไวยากรณ์ ชื่อเรื่องคงเดิม只改语法。标题不动。

This entry is in English only.บันทึกนี้มีแต่ภาษาอังกฤษ此篇仅有英文。

  1. “We may have suffered more in our own imagination than in reality.” (This quote is credited to the great political figure Seneca);
  2. We were born without anything, not even the clothes we love. So, anything that we’ve got in life today, no matter how small, is already a bonus;
  3. Don’t let your interpretation of anything, especially a side of reality, affect your wellbeing;
  4. 90% of life suck; that’s the reality. But the other 10% is something really really worth living your life for;
  5. The ability of breath when suffocated is the true meaning of optimism;
  6. Don’t try too hard. A wise sage once said, “When you want nothing, everything is yours. So, it’s best when you do nothing. The second best is not wanting anything in return when you do something;
  7. Do good things. Don’t do bad things. With the hope that, eventually, you’ll get to do nothing at all. There’s a true sense of peace when you’re not obligated to do anything and for anyone;
  8. When things get hard, remember the good things that had happened in your life, re-live them in your imagination, and use them as emotional energy to live. Giving up living before your time comes (and believe me, it will come) is a waste of opportunity to see things the way they really are;
  9. At least, every once in a while, try to be the best version of yourself;
  10. I don’t know what passion is. What makes more sense is to know what you’re good at doing, and what you love doing. It’d be awesome if these two things are the same, but if they’re not, then you’re going to choose what to do. That decision is what I think of as passion;
  11. You’re going to die one day, and it could be today or tomorrow. If you feel as though your job is bullshit. Don’t let it be the last thing that you do. Giving it up to live in the woods is better than spending even a minute doing bullshit job; and,
  12. Don’t explain yourself to someone who is already prepared to come up with an argument to rebuke it.
